# v0.50.0 – the grade measured from your battery's data

Works together with wolta.se switching to the data-driven benchmark. The grade is now computed against the capacity and efficiency Wolta measures from your battery's state of charge, not against the battery details you entered.

- **The grade sensor tells you whether your grade is compared with others.** `sensor.wolta_optimisation_grade` has a new attribute `benchmark` with `comparable` (true or false) and `reasons`. A grade that is not comparable is a grade of your own: it is computed and shown, but it is left out of the comparison with other installations. The most common reason is `no_soc`, meaning no state-of-charge sensor is selected.
- **The battery value sensor stays on the same figure as the economics.** After the switch, the grade can cover a different period than payback and IRR. `sensor.wolta_battery_value_per_year` keeps following the figure the economics are anchored on, so it does not jump when wolta.se switches over. It can therefore differ from the "You captured" figure next to the grade on wolta.se.
- **A state-of-charge sensor is now required.** Setup and Reconfigure ask you to pick one, or to tick **My battery has no SoC sensor**. Picking a sensor uploads up to 365 days of its history right away. The economics still use the battery details you entered.
- **New repair: "Wolta needs your battery's state-of-charge sensor".** It appears on plants with a battery that have neither picked a sensor nor ticked the box, and it replaces the earlier optional repair. If you had ignored the old one, you are asked again.
- **What to expect.** Plants that have neither picked a sensor nor ticked the box will stop being able to upload data in a coming version. No date is set.
- **What to do.** Pick your battery's state-of-charge sensor under Reconfigure (three-dot menu on the Wolta integration), or tick the box if the battery has none.
- **The control system follows wolta.se.** If you change or backdate your control system on wolta.se, the integration now picks up the change. Before, it kept the value from setup, and re-authenticating sent that old value back, which could undo a switch you had declared on wolta.se.
- **New repair: "Wolta needs your battery's control system".** It appears on plants that have no control system set at Wolta, or Other without a name. Pick one in the repair; you can change it later on wolta.se.
- **Your own grade window.** If you set a grade window on wolta.se, the optimisation grade sensor shows the grade for that window, and `period_start` and `period_end` show its dates. The new Comparison grade sensor shows the grade over the last 12 full months, the one compared with other installations, with its percentile and the `benchmark` details. When a window is set, those details are no longer on the optimisation grade sensor.
