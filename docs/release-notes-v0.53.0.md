# v0.53.0 – purchase price sensor and reduced energy tax

A new sensor shows what a kilowatt-hour costs you to buy, including VAT, and the tariff settings gain the two things it needs: a reduced-energy-tax answer and a required fixed part for percentage grid fees. The sensor is information only: it never affects the optimisation grade, the economics or anything the integration uploads. **It needs a Wolta server with API 0.118.0 or later** (older servers answer 404 for the new endpoint, so the sensor stays `unavailable`; everything else works as before).

- **New sensor: Purchase price.** `sensor.wolta_purchase_price` (English instances; `sensor.wolta_inkopspris` on Swedish ones) is the price of the current quarter-hour in SEK/kWh **including VAT**: the spot price plus your electricity supplier's markup, plus the energy tax (after the reduced energy tax, if you have it), plus your variable grid fee, with VAT on all of it. The server works it out from the tariff you saved under Configure → Settings → Tariffs, the same one the grade uses. It exists for SE1–SE4 and it is **disabled by default**: enable it under Settings → Devices & services → Wolta → entities.
- **What it does not include.** Fixed charges that cannot be written as a price per kWh: the subscription fees, the fuse fee and the power tariff. Contracts with a fixed price per kWh are not covered either.
- **Attributes.** `prices` is a list of `{start, purchase, spot}` points for today and, once published, tomorrow, all in SEK/kWh. `purchase` is including VAT, `spot` is the spot price **excluding VAT**, the same figure as `sensor.wolta_spot_price`, which stays excluding VAT and is unchanged. A quarter without a price has `purchase: null`. `components` shows what the price is built from (your markup and grid fee, your reduced-energy-tax answer, the energy tax per year after any reduction, the VAT factor), `reason` says why there is no price, and `tomorrow_complete` says whether tomorrow is fully published. `prices` is not stored by the recorder.
- **The state follows the quarter-hour on its own.** It is worked out locally from `prices`, so it changes on the quarter without waiting for the next poll.
- **When there is no price, `reason` says what to do.** The state is then `unknown`, not `unavailable`: Home Assistant hides all attributes of an unavailable entity, and `reason` is the point.
  - `tariff_not_set`: set both the grid fee and the supplier markup (`0` counts as set).
  - `grid_fixed_missing`: your grid fee is a percentage without a fixed part; enter the fixed part (`0` if you only have a percentage).
  - `energy_tax_unconfirmed`: answer the new *Reduced energy tax* question (SE1–SE3 only).
  - `fx_missing`: Wolta has no EUR/SEK exchange rate right now (it also holds back the spot price); nothing to do, it returns with the rate.
  - `zone_not_supported`: the plant's zone is not SE1–SE4.
- **`unavailable` means no data.** The Wolta server could not be reached, answered an error (a server before API 0.118.0 answers 404) or the data is out of date. The spot price sensors and the rest of the integration are not affected. The failure is logged once at info level when it starts and once when it is over.
- **One extra request, only when you use it.** While the sensor is enabled, the integration reads the prices from the Wolta server once per 15-minute poll, concurrently with the spot price request, with the plant token in the `Authorization` header. With the sensor disabled (the default) nothing extra is requested. Saving a changed tariff under Configure refreshes the sensor straight away.
- **New option: Reduced energy tax.** Under Configure → Settings → Tariffs (SE1–SE3): *Not set*, *No* or *Yes*. Some northern municipalities have a reduced energy tax of 9.6 öre/kWh excluding VAT (all of Norrbotten, Västerbotten and Jämtland, and Sollefteå, Ånge, Örnsköldsvik, Ljusdal, Malung-Sälen, Mora, Orsa, Älvdalen and Torsby; source: Skatteverket). The municipalities do not follow the price zones, so Wolta asks. *Not set* keeps the grade exactly as before; *Yes* lowers the import price used for the grade and the economics by that amount. The purchase price stays without a price until you answer.
- **A percentage grid fee now needs its fixed part.** If you enter *Grid fee, percent of spot*, the fixed grid fee must be filled in too (`0` if you only have a percentage). The options form says so instead of sending a save the server would refuse.
- **New repair: "Wolta: your grid fee needs its fixed part".** It appears when your saved grid fee is a percentage without a fixed part, and clears itself once you have entered it. Re-authentication asks for the fixed part as well in that case. Not raised in view-only mode, where the tariff is set on wolta.se.
- **Translations.** The new sensor, option, error and repair are in English and Swedish.
