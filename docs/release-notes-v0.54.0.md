# v0.54.0 – sale price, grid benefit, electricity contract and forecast price sensors

Three new sensors show what you are paid for the electricity you sell, and what you pay and are paid in the coming days. The tariff settings gain the grid benefit (*nätnytta*) as a field of its own and your electricity contract (quarter-hour, monthly or fixed price, a change after your first answer applying from today), the options form shows the time periods, the contract history and the history of what you are paid for sold electricity that you keep on wolta.se, and the export premium becomes what it should always have been, your electricity retailer's terms. The sensors are information only: they never affect the optimisation grade, the economics or anything the integration uploads. **It needs a Wolta server with API 0.119.0 or later.** An older server has no sale price: the sale sensors then stay at `unknown`, the purchase price works as before, and the new tariff fields and repairs do nothing.

- **New sensor: Sale price.** `sensor.wolta_sale_price` (English instances; `sensor.wolta_forsaljningspris` on Swedish ones) is the price you are paid for a sold kilowatt-hour in the current quarter-hour, in SEK/kWh **excluding VAT** (a private person does not charge VAT; the purchase price stays including VAT). It is the spot price plus the grid benefit of that quarter, plus your retailer's terms, plus the tax reduction for micro-production of the quarter's calendar year (none from 1 January 2026). The server works it out with the same function the grade uses for the export price, so the sensor and the grade cannot disagree. SE1–SE4 only, **disabled by default**.
- **Attributes.** `prices` of the sale sensors is a list of `{start, sale, spot, grid_benefit}` points for today and, once published, tomorrow, and `prices` of the purchase sensors `{start, purchase, spot, grid_fee}`. `grid_fee` is the quarter's whole variable grid fee, its percentage of spot included, and `grid_benefit` the quarter's whole grid benefit, its percentage of spot included, both in SEK/kWh excluding VAT, so you can see why a price differs between quarters (a time period with a price of its own, set on wolta.se); they are `null` when you have not entered the base price and from a server that does not send them. `components` shows what the price is built from (`grid_benefit_ore`, `export_extra_ore`, `export_extra_pct`, `export_credit_ore` per year, today's contract and the time periods as they are applied), `reason` says why there is no price and `tomorrow_complete` whether tomorrow is fully published. `prices` is not stored by the recorder.
- **When there is no sale price, `reason` says what to do.** The state is then `unknown`, as on the purchase sensor, and the two prices are available independently of each other. `grid_benefit_not_set`: enter your grid benefit; `fx_missing`: Wolta has no exchange rate right now; `zone_not_supported`: the zone is not SE1–SE4.
- **New sensors: Purchase price with forecast and Sale price with forecast.** The same state as the plain sensors, but `prices` also carries Wolta's forecast for up to three days ahead (the days without a published price), with `forecast: true`/`false` on every point and `horizon` (1, 2 or 3) on forecast points, in time order and with no timestamp twice. They are separate sensors so that nobody reading `prices` on the plain sensors suddenly gets forecast points, as with *Spot price with forecast*. `forecast_days` lists date, horizon and issue time per forecast day, and `forecast_source` and `attribution` credit the forecast sources (`forecast_source` is `null` without forecast days). A forecast price is a rough shape of the coming days, not a price to rely on to the öre; the forecast never feeds the grade.
- **One request, only when you use it.** The four price sensors (purchase, sale and the two forecast variants) share one read of the Wolta server per 15-minute poll. With all four disabled (the default) nothing is requested. A failed poll keeps the last answer for as long as it covers the current quarter-hour, also when only its sale price (or only its purchase price) was available. The purchase sensor now also becomes unavailable when a kept answer without a purchase price stops covering the current quarter-hour.
- **Grid benefit fields.** Under Configure → Settings → Tariffs: *Grid benefit* (excluding VAT, öre/kWh; `0` is a valid answer; the field is named as on wolta.se) and, for the percentage part, *Sold electricity, percent of spot* (the former export compensation percentage; in SE1–SE4 it is the percentage part of the grid benefit). The amount is shown in SE1–SE4 only and lies between −20 and 50 öre; the percentage is shown in every zone and lies between −100 and 100. A percentage needs its fixed part, as the grid fee percentage does, and the options form refuses the save and says so, both at the field and at the top of the form.
- **Time periods are set on wolta.se and shown here.** A network operator can charge a different grid fee, or pay a different grid benefit, at some times (a time-of-use tariff). Those time periods, each with months, all days or weekdays with a holiday rule, hours and a price of its own, are set on wolta.se (*Configure → Link to a wolta.se account* if your plant is not linked yet). The options form shows how many periods each side has and never sends or changes them; *Grid fee (transfer)* and *Grid benefit* are the prices outside the periods and are required while a side has periods. The purchase price, the sale price, the grade and the economy figures use the period's price in the quarters it covers. The percentage of spot in the grid fee is always counted on the market's spot price.
- **Electricity contract.** *Electricity contract* is the contract you have today: *Quarter-hour price* (the standard, as before), *Monthly price* or *Fixed price*. While Wolta has no contract type stored for you (and no contract history), the form starts on *Not stated*: Wolta counts quarter-hour price until you choose, and your first choice, *Quarter-hour price* included, applies to all of your history. Saving with *Not stated* sends nothing. *Monthly price* is the plain average of the month's spot prices (quarter-hour from October 2025, hourly before); it is set after the month is over, so the purchase price sensor has no quarter-hour price then (`reason: monthly_price_contract`). Your retailer's cost for volume weighting is not part of the average: put it in the markup. *Fixed price* (excluding VAT, öre/kWh, the retailer's price including its markup); the purchase price is built on it instead of spot plus markup (the energy tax, the grid fee and VAT are added as usual). A fixed price carries no markup: choosing it removes the markup, and the form refuses a markup typed next to it. **A change applies from today**: Wolta keeps a contract history, adds the change as a new contract dated today and keeps counting earlier days with the contract you had then; two first answers apply to all of your history instead: the first contract type you choose, if Wolta has none stored for you yet, and a markup entered for the first time without changing the contract type. Payback, IRR and the purchase price use today's contract. A contract that changed on another day, and corrections back in time, are made in the contract list on wolta.se, which the options form summarises (how many contracts, since when today's applies). Wolta counts per quarter-hour, so a contract priced per hour should be entered as *Quarter-hour price* and can differ within the hour from what your retailer charges; contracts that mix fixed and spot prices are not supported.
- **Forms and errors.** The fields are named as on wolta.se (*Grid fee (transfer)*, *Grid benefit*, *Electricity contract*). The options form says what is wrong before it sends (a fixed price without the Fixed price contract or the other way round, a markup next to a fixed price, a base price cleared while it has time periods) and shows each of the server's refusals at the field and at the top of the form, with the Tariffs section open. Re-authentication and linking carry today's contract too (never a markup with a fixed price), but never the time periods or any history, which stay on the server.
- **The sale side has a history too.** Your network operator's grid benefit normally changes with its price list at the turn of the year, and your retailer's terms when you switch contract, so Wolta keeps a history of each (on wolta.se, as for the contract). The options form keeps the same fields, *Grid benefit*, *Sold electricity, percent of spot* and *Retailer's terms for sold electricity*, and edits today's values: **after your first answer, a change applies from today**, and the grade and the economy figures keep counting earlier days with what applied then, while the sale price sensor, payback and IRR use today's values. Your first answer applies to all of your history instead: for the retailer's terms the first value other than 0 (an empty field is stored as 0, so 0 is not counted as an answer; if you really had 0 until now, correct the date of the change on wolta.se), and for the grid benefit the very first amount, after which every change of the amount, the percentage or the time periods applies from today. A change on another day and corrections back in time are made in the lists on wolta.se, which the options form summarises (how many entries each history has, since when today's applies) and never sends; an untouched save sends nothing. While the grid benefit has a history, *Grid benefit* cannot be cleared, because today's entry needs a base price: the form says so at the field and at the top of the form, before it sends, and shows the server's refusal the same way. Cleared retailer's terms are plain spot from today.
- **New repair: "Wolta: a time period of your grid fee looks too high".** The same warning as for the grid fee, for the prices of the grid fee's time periods, with a limit of its own that the Wolta server sets (currently 80 öre per kWh), because a real time-of-use tariff reaches 61 öre excluding VAT. The periods are corrected on wolta.se. It clears itself once the server finds the prices plausible. Not raised in view-only mode.
- **Export premium is now your retailer's terms.** The field is now called *Retailer's terms for sold electricity*: what your electricity retailer pays (+) or deducts (−) per sold kWh on top of the spot price, `0` for plain spot. The formula is unchanged; only its meaning is, because many people entered their grid benefit there. *Export compensation, percent of spot* is now called *Sold electricity, percent of spot* and, in SE1–SE4, is the percentage part of the grid benefit.
- **New repair: "Wolta: separate your grid benefit from your retailer's terms".** Raised in SE1–SE4 when you have a retailer's-terms amount other than 0 and no grid benefit: Wolta adds a standard 5 öre of grid benefit on top, so a figure that was really your grid benefit is counted twice. Nothing changes until you save; the grade is calculated as before until then. It clears itself once the grid benefit is set.
- **New repair: "Wolta: your grid benefit percentage needs its fixed part".** Raised when you have a grid benefit percentage without the fixed part (Wolta would add a standard 5 öre as the fixed part, which looks like your figure but is a guess). It clears itself once the fixed part is entered. Neither repair is raised in view-only mode, where the tariff is set on wolta.se.
- **Both fields are saved together.** The form shows the grid benefit and the retailer's terms side by side, and saving a grid benefit also saves the retailer's terms (`0` if you left them empty), so that you have seen and confirmed both. The grid benefit fields are shown in SE1–SE4 only, and changes made on wolta.se show up in Home Assistant like the other tariff fields.
- **Translations.** The new sensors, fields, errors and repairs are in English and Swedish.
