# v0.51.0 – spot price sensors with forecast

Two new sensors show the day-ahead spot price for the Swedish price zones SE1–SE4, one of them with a forecast for the part of tomorrow that has no published price yet. They are information only: they never affect the optimisation grade, the economics or anything the integration uploads.

- **New sensor: Spot price.** `sensor.wolta_spot_price` shows the spot price of the current quarter-hour in SEK/kWh excluding VAT. The state is worked out locally and flips on the quarter-hour without waiting for the next poll. The attribute `prices` lists today's and, once published, tomorrow's prices as `{start, price}` points. `vat_factor` is the Swedish VAT multiplier (1.25, 25 %) and `tomorrow_complete` says whether tomorrow's prices are all out.
- **New sensor: Spot price with forecast (disabled by default).** `sensor.wolta_spot_price_with_forecast` has the same state, but `prices` also carries a forecast for tomorrow's quarters that have no published price yet, each point marked `forecast: true` or `false`. Enable it under Settings → Devices & services → Wolta → entities.
- **How good the forecast is.** The attributes `forecast_issued_at` and `forecast_accuracy` describe it. Wolta measures the forecast that was shown before 13:00 against the prices that were then published. With at least 14 measured days, `forecast_accuracy.basis` is `wolta`, and `mae_ore_kwh` and `peak_mae_ore_kwh` are Wolta's own errors in öre/kWh excluding VAT over the last 30 days. Before that, `basis` is `upstream` and `upstream_mae_ore_kwh` is the predictor project's own backtest error for your zone, about 18–26 öre/kWh, measured with actual weather, so the real-world error is somewhat larger.
- **Prices are not stored by the recorder.** The `prices` attribute is excluded from the recorder, so it does not fill the database. Read it from the state in a template or an automation.
- **Swedish zones only.** The prices are converted to SEK with Swedish VAT, so the sensors are created for SE1–SE4 only. For other zones nothing is created and no price requests are made. The zone is read at setup: if you change it with *Correct the price zone* between a Swedish and a non-Swedish zone, the sensors stay as they were until the integration is reloaded.
- **Never affects the grade.** The price sensors have their own data source and read nothing the grade, the upload or the profile use. If the price endpoint fails, only these two sensors become unavailable; the existing sensors are not touched.
- **Source.** The `attribution` attribute comes from the server and is in Swedish. The spot price sensor names the source of the prices only. The forecast sensor, while a forecast is present, also credits the forecast sources, with a link to [EpexPredictor](https://github.com/b3nn0/EpexPredictor), energy-charts and Open-Meteo.
- **When the forecast appears.** The spot price sensor works as soon as you update. The forecast appears once wolta.se has deployed the server side; until then the forecast sensor shows the published prices only.
- **EMHASS example.** The README has an example template that turns the `prices` attribute into a price list for EMHASS.
