# v0.55.2 – clearer errors when Wolta refuses a setting

When you save the Wolta settings and wolta.se refuses a value, the form now says why. Before, some refusals showed "check your internet connection", even though the connection was fine.

- **The purchase date, for a plant built in stages:** if your battery came years after the solar panels, the purchase date is the first stage's date, and the later stages are set on wolta.se. A later date typed here is refused, and the form now says so at the purchase date field. The field's description says it too.
- **Any other refusal:** the form shows Wolta's own reason instead of a connection error.
- **A real connection problem** still shows the connection error.

You do not need to do anything. Update when it suits you.
